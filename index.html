Discover Wellness Ridge

A Master-Planned Community in Clermont, Florida

Wellness Ridge is a thoughtfully designed master-planned community nestled in the heart of Clermont, Florida. This neighborhood was created with an emphasis on outdoor living and a nature-centered lifestyle, making it ideal for those who appreciate both comfort and connection to the natural world.

Community Amenities

  • 🌳 Multi-use walking and biking trails
  • 🏞 Open green spaces and parks
  • 🏊 Community pool and recreation areas
  • 📍 Near Lake Louisa State Park

Residents of Wellness Ridge enjoy convenient access to outdoor recreation at nearby Lake Louisa State Park, local shops and dining in downtown Clermont, and the world-famous attractions of the greater Orlando area—all while returning home to a peaceful, family-friendly environment.
